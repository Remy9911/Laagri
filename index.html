PLAY ON AIRCONSOLE
Opens AirConsole in this browser. Chrome on a laptop connected to the TV works best.
  1. 1
    Big screen: connect the laptop to the TV (HDMI), press Play above and go fullscreen with F11. Click once on the page to turn on sound.
  2. 2
    Phones: everyone opens airconsole.com (or the AirConsole app), types the code from the TV and turns the phone sideways.
  3. 3
    Lobby: vote a track and tap READY. The first phone is the host and sets battle length (2/3/5 min) and CPU drivers.
  4. 4
    Drive: cars go by themselves. Steer with ◀ ▶, BRAKE for tight corners, BOOST fires the item you grabbed from a ? box (turbo, mega turbo, rocket, oil, shield). Yellow arrows on the road are free boosts.
  5. 5
    Win: the camera follows the leader. Fall off the TV screen and you're out. Last car left wins the heat; everyone restarts there, with the heat winner at the back of the grid. Most heat wins when the clock runs out wins the battle.
